##Studyō
This is studyō, hopefully your new study partner, it was designed to help students get to sit and actually study (mainly made for myself since i always get distracted) I made this with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I created a clean, minimal space where you can customize your own focus sessions, track your tasks, and actually get stuff. You can even write your deadlines in the agenda. I  really hope it helps you stress a little less and study a lot smarter ;)
